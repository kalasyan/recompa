# 🚀 Recompa 0.4.1

A polish release: every style now works in portrait, you can sort the library, and many details of the styles have been fixed.

## ✨ What's new

- **Sort the library:** By original game name (ignoring accents and articles), recently added, recently updated or best rated. Change it with the Select button, the sort button (it opens a small menu) or in Settings; your choice is remembered. "Default" keeps the usual order.
- **Portrait screens in every style:** Stacked headers, port pages with the art above the text and full-screen settings. The app follows the device's rotation without restarting.
- **New ports by day:** The recently added section and the "New" badge show the ports added on the last day that had new ports.

## 🛠 Fixes

- **Showcase:** The filter tabs get all the room beside the name, and the one picked with L1/R1 always stays in view. The box a touch drag stops on becomes the selected one.
- **Arcade:** The cover a touch drag stops on becomes the selected one, and the cover badge fits on one line.
- **List:** More room for the platform name and the header buttons.
- **Classic and the other styles:** The sort and update-all buttons no longer cover titles or tabs.
- Focus returns to the library when Settings closes.
- After changing the order, the list starts from the top.
